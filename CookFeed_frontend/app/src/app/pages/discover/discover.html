How Cookfeed works

  1. Watch

    Find a recipe in a short cooking video. Every step is marked, so you can jump to the part you are on.

  2. Plan

    Save the recipes you like, or buy a creator's meal plan, and put them on your week.

  3. Shop once

    Cookfeed adds everything up into one shopping list, sorted by aisle. One trip covers the week.
