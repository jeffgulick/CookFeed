Questions and answers

The short version of how Cookfeed works.

What is the difference between a meal plan and a collection?

A meal plan puts recipes on days and meals, such as Monday dinner, and gives you one shopping list for the whole plan. A plan can use a recipe more than once: Family Week on a Budget has 5 recipes and 6 meals, because the overnight oats are on two mornings. A collection is a set of recipes a creator chose to go together, with no days attached.

What do I get when I buy one?

Every recipe in it, its cooking video, and one shopping list scaled to the people you cook for. Every recipe in a plan is premium: it is never in the free feed, and it opens once you own the plan, marked "Yours through" the plan. Each creator's free recipes are in Discover and on their page. You keep exactly what you bought, even if the creator changes the plan later.

Why is there no Add to cart on my own plan?

Creators cannot buy their own plans, or review them. They can buy any other creator's plan, the same as any home cook.

Who can write a review?

Only cooks who bought the plan, once each, under their account name. So every review comes from someone who actually cooked it. You can remove your review and write a new one.

Can I buy more than one copy?

No. A plan is yours once you own it, so the cart holds one copy of each.

Why does the admin account see no cart or Save buttons?

The admin account runs the site and holds no other role, so it does not shop, save or review.

Is this the real store?

It is a prototype. The accounts, plans, recipes and reviews are the project database's rows, and there are no passwords: pick an account to see the store as that person. Carts, saves, orders and reviews are kept in this browser, and Reset prototype data puts everything back.
