Questions and answers

The short version of how Cookfeed works.

What is the difference between a meal plan and a collection?

A meal plan puts recipes on days and meals, such as Monday dinner. A plan can use a recipe more than once: Family Week on a Budget has 5 recipes and 6 meals, because the overnight oats are on two mornings. A collection is a set of recipes a creator chose to go together, with no days attached.

What do I get when I buy one?

Every recipe in it and its cooking video. Every recipe in a plan is premium: it is never in the free feed or on the Recipes page, and it opens once you own the plan, marked "Yours through" the plan. Each creator's free recipes are in Discover and on their page. You keep exactly what you bought, even if the creator changes the plan later or Cookfeed takes a recipe out of the store.

Why is there no Add to cart on my own plan?

Creators cannot buy their own plans, or review them. They can buy any other creator's plan, the same as any home cook.

Who can write a review?

Only cooks who bought the plan, once each, under their account name. So every review comes from someone who actually cooked it. You can delete your review and write a new one, unless Cookfeed hid it.

Can I buy more than one copy?

No. You buy each plan once, and it stays yours, so the cart holds one copy of each.

How do I plan my week?

Press Add to my week on a recipe and pick the day, the meal and the people, or press it on a plan you own to put every meal on its own day. In My kitchen, Plan it on a saved recipe does the same. To change a meal, press Move and pick another day, meal or number of people, or drag it by its ⠿ handle onto another day and meal. The kitchen opens on this week; Week after and Week before move between weeks.

How do I get a shopping list?

You shop from a single list at a time. Press Shop this recipe on a recipe, Shop this plan on a plan you own, or Shop this week in My kitchen, and it goes straight onto your list, which is made for you if you have none. A message says what was added, with Undo and Open list. A recipe is never on the list twice. Saving a recipe or buying a plan never adds to the list by itself.

What can I do with my list?

Open it on your phone at the store. Press Have it on anything already at home (and put it back if you need it), change the people for a recipe, or add more. Press Start shopping and tick each thing as it goes in your basket, then Done shopping. Or send it to Instacart or Favor, and press Items arrived when it comes; a sent list cannot change. Finished lists are kept under Past lists, where Shop again makes a new list from one when you have no list going.

I am a creator. Where do I add a recipe or a plan?

In My studio, in the navigation when you are signed in as a creator. A recipe starts as a draft only you can open; publish it when it is ready, free for anyone to save or premium for buyers of a plan. A plan or collection is for sale as soon as you create it from your published premium recipes. Retiring a plan takes it off the store; everyone who bought it keeps it. The studio also shows your sales and the reviews of your plans.

Why does the admin account see no cart or Save buttons?

The admin account runs the site and holds no other role, so it does not shop, save or review. Its Admin console hides or restores reviews, verifies or suspends creators, retires plans, takes recipes out of the store, refunds or cancels orders, and looks up accounts. Every change it makes is written to an audit log.

Is this the real store?

It is a prototype. The accounts, plans, recipes and reviews are the project database's rows, and there are no passwords: pick an account to see the store as that person. Carts, saves, orders and reviews are kept in this browser, and Reset prototype data puts everything back.
